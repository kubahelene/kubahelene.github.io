02 — ABOUT

I'm Helene.
And I'm part of
the sport I photograph.

I know how much can go into just a few minutes on stage. The work, the nerves, the excitement and how much it means to finally step on stage again.

I'm not always the one behind the camera - sometimes I'm the one helping you out backstage and sometimes I'm the one on stage.

I'm part of this world myself. I know the depths of prep, the nerves, the short conversation whilst getting the first layer of tan, and the people who support each other when it truly matters. Those are the moments I care about just as much as anything that happens on stage.

Studying architecture has shaped the way I see space, light and composition. Being an athlete has shaped the way I see the people in front of my camera.

I don't just photograph what happened. I want people to look back at their photos and remember what it felt like to be there. To remember that feeling.
